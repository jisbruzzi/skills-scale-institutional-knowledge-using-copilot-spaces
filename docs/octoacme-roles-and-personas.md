# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads ensure quality standards are met through test planning, execution, and validation. They work with developers and product managers to define acceptance criteria, create test plans, and validate that features meet quality benchmarks.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Design and execute unit, integration, and end-to-end tests
- Identify and triage quality issues and regressions
- Collaborate with developers on testability and test automation
- Validate features against acceptance criteria before release
- Track quality metrics and report test coverage

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality features with minimal production defects
- Enable fast, reliable releases through automation
- Provide clear quality visibility to stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and retrospectives
- Test results and quality reports
- Defect tracking and prioritization
- Acceptance criteria clarification

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide system design, technical strategy, and architectural decisions. They work with developers and product managers to ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and align with technical standards.

### Responsibilities
- Design technical solutions and system architecture
- Guide technical decisions and trade-offs
- Mentor developers and conduct technical reviews
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Ensure alignment with company technical standards
- Define scalability, security, and performance requirements

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and risk
- Enable team growth and knowledge sharing

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code reviews and technical guidance
- Risk and dependency identification

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business direction, governance, and authorization. They represent business priorities and ensure projects align with strategic objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and strategic alignment
- Approve project charter and major decisions
- Ensure adequate resource allocation
- Escalate and resolve organizational blockers
- Receive status updates and reports
- Participate in milestone reviews and gates

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain visibility into progress and risks
- Align team efforts with business strategy

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and reports
- Milestone reviews and decision gates
- Escalation and risk communication

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads ensure that projects meet security and compliance requirements. They review designs, test implementations, and help the team build security into the development lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review security requirements and threat models
- Guide secure design and implementation practices
- Review code and designs for security vulnerabilities
- Plan and execute security testing
- Ensure compliance with security standards and regulations
- Incident response and post-incident reviews

### Goals
- Deliver secure, compliant solutions
- Build security awareness and practices across the team
- Reduce security vulnerabilities and compliance risks

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and design discussions
- Vulnerability reports and remediation tracking
- Compliance and audit coordination

---

## DevOps/Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Release Engineers manage deployment pipelines, infrastructure, and release processes. They enable reliable, fast releases and maintain system observability.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure
- Plan and execute production deployments
- Manage rollback procedures and incident response
- Configure monitoring, logging, and alerting
- Ensure deployment reliability and performance
- Document deployment procedures and runbooks

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, low-risk releases
- Maintain high system availability and observability
- Reduce deployment lead time and errors

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment coordination
- Incident response and post-mortems
- Infrastructure and pipeline updates

---

## Design/UX Lead

### Role Summary
Design and UX Leads shape the user experience through research, design, and validation. They work with product and development teams to ensure solutions are usable and delightful.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Design user interfaces and user experiences
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design systems
- Collaborate with developers on implementation
- Validate designs through user feedback
- Maintain design consistency and standards

### Goals
- Deliver usable, delightful user experiences
- Reduce user friction and support burden
- Maximize user adoption and satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions
- Usability testing results
- Design specification and collaboration with developers

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business needs and technical solutions. They gather requirements, analyze business processes, and ensure solutions address business problems effectively.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements
- Analyze current-state processes and pain points
- Define desired future-state processes
- Create requirements specifications and acceptance criteria
- Validate solutions meet business needs
- Support stakeholder communication and change management

### Goals
- Ensure solutions address actual business problems
- Minimize rework through clear requirements
- Support successful adoption and change management

### Typical Communication
- Requirements gathering interviews and workshops
- Requirements documentation and specification
- Stakeholder updates and impact analysis
